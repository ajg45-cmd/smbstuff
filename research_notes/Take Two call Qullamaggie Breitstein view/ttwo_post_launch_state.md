## Launch outcome: reviews, sales, technical problems, online mode, PC

### Takeaway
GTA VI launched with a 99 Metacritic score (tied for the highest ever) and third-party estimates of about 24.6 million units and $2.36 billion on day one, but Take-Two and Rockstar had published no official sales figure as of the reports found. Launch problems were limited to PlayStation Network login errors; there is no PC version and no announced online mode.
