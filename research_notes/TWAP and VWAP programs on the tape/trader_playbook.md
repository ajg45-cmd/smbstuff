## 1. SMB Capital (Bellafiore, Spencer, desk traders): Stocks In Play, held bid, VWAP, trend trades, when the buyer is done, adding size

### Takeaway
SMB's public material supports a consistent chain: trade only Stocks In Play (catalyst plus relative volume), look for a clean held bid as evidence of a large buyer, tier size up as confirming data appears (VWAP is one such guide), treat a dropped bid as an exit that is re-entered if the stock does not actually go down, and pre-write "Reasons2Sell" lists. What I could not retrieve is any SMB text that explicitly says "long above VWAP / short below" or calls VWAP "the institutional benchmark" — that framing is common elsewhere but unverified for SMB here.

### Cited Findings

**Stocks In Play**
- [A, primary] A 2023 SMB post lists the criteria for a Stock In Play as: catalyst (news, technical or price) scored 8 or better out of 10, liquidity, range, and relative volume above 3. A premarket move of +/-3% with volume counts as a price catalyst. — [SMB: 15 Best Day Trading Stocks YTD (2023)](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/?p=56451)
- [A, primary] SMB's RVOL post defines RVOL as current volume versus normal volume for the same time of day; "If RVOL is less than 1 it is not In Play... If RVOL is above 2 it is In Play." Note the threshold conflict (2 here, 3 in the 2023 post). — [SMB: Relative Volume (RVOL) Defined and Used](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/relative-volume-rvol-defined-and-how-we-use-it)
- [A, primary] An older post drawn from The PlayBook on "Super Stocks In Play" also uses stock +/-3% with increased premarket volume. — [SMB: Super Stocks In Play](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/?p=30132)

**Held bid / held offer and the large buyer**
- [A, primary] SMB: a held bid above an important support or resistance level gets the desk's attention, and "the clean held bid often foreshadows a big move." — [SMB: The Clean Held Bids Matter Most](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/the-clean-held-bids-matter-most)
- [A, primary] SMB PlayBook post: when a bid is being held and you are thinking of getting long, prepare for the bid to drop; if the stock "does not really go down and rebids at the same price, consider getting back in." — [SMB: The SMB PlayBook: Compiling our best trades](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/the-smb-playbook-compiling-our-best-trades)
- [A, primary] SMB describes a trader exiting when a bid was hit, then re-entering when the stock did not fall much further, calling it an accumulation pattern. — [SMB: Foreshadowing on the Tape](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/foreshadowing-on-the-tape)
- [A, secondary] A reader's review of One Good Trade summarises: a held bid is a buyer repeatedly showing far more size than is displayed at one price while the bid does not drop (a hidden large order). If the bid drops because a seller hit it and a buyer returns quickly, that is a shakeout and you may rebuy; exit if the offer holds below support, if the bid under support gives way, or if an aggressive seller takes out the level. — [Book review of One Good Trade](https://whatheheckaboom.wordpress.com/2012/05/01/book-review-of-one-good-trade-by-mike-bellafiore/)
- [A, primary] Bellafiore says he does not trust the trend of a stock until some time has elapsed after the open, in a post about bid buying in MCD (excerpt truncated). — [SMB blog post 949](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/949)
- [A, primary] SMB video write-up (Peloton trade): two desk traders discuss a trade where "one of the best exits was spotted with Tape Reading" — the tape slowing into a level — and where the short-term trade was more profitable than the swing. Exact exit rule not readable from the excerpt. — [SMB: How to use Tape Reading to determine one of the most profitable exits](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/how-to-use-tape-reading-to-determine-one-of-the-most-profitable-exits)
- [A, secondary, forum] EliteTrader thread on SMB-style tape reading: a held bid/offer gives a low-risk scalp whose exit is the held bid dropping or the held offer lifting. — [EliteTrader thread](https://www.elitetrader.com/et/threads/question-about-reading-the-tape.208199/)

**VWAP in SMB material**
- [A, primary] SMB Midday Recap post (25 Oct 2013): covers an execution principle for breakout stocks, a "second-day VWAP support play" in CAT, and stocks with "independent order flow" (FIO as example). — [SMB: Trade Execution — VWAP and independent order flow](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/trade-execution-how-to-use-vwap-and-the-principle-of-independent-order-flow-video); [SMB VWAP tag archive](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/tag/vwap)
- [A, primary] SMB's "Fashionably Late Scalp" cheat sheet (2024): enter long as soon as an upsloping 9 EMA crosses a flat-to-downsloping VWAP (mirror for shorts); stop and target are set by a "measured move"; skip if price is choppy or if the 9 EMA stays flat for more than 15 minutes between the turn and the entry; windows listed are 10:00-10:45 am ET and mid-day; SMB claims a 60% win rate (firm's own claim, no data shown). — [SMB: Fashionably Late Scalp Cheat Sheet (PDF)](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/The-Fashionably-Late-Scalp-Cheat-Sheet.pdf)
- [A, primary] SMB swing guide: the Day 2 continuation play follows a powerful directional Day 1 move on a breakout or fundamental catalyst that closes near the high of the range; Day 2 entries are framed around Day 1 support levels and where the two-day VWAP forms. — [SMB: Ultimate Swing Trading Guide](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/the-ultimate-swing-trading-guide-for-beginners-developing-traders)
- [A, secondary, provenance unclear] A slide deck summarising The PlayBook says "VWAP is best as an indicator when it is curving to the downside." — [The playbook summary (Google Slides)](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1NZV1JGhKXawqJ08dUdVPViufldl3CDUjgB8hadBWEho/htmlpresent)

**Trend-following a program: Trend Trend, Trade2Hold, 2nd Day Play, Changing Fundamentals**
- [A, primary] SMB PlayBook webinar deck: "The strongest pattern we are looking for is a Trend-Trend trade, where a stock is trending on both long and short term time frames" — the best kind of 2nd Day Play; such plays need not occur only on the second day. — [SMB: 2nd Day Play presentation (SlideShare)](https://www.slideshare.net/smbcapital/edu-2nd-day-play-presentation)
- [A, secondary] The PlayBook summary deck separates a "Move2Move" trade (very short term) from a "Trend Trend Trade, Trade2Hold, where we are trying to capture the real move", and says to define the expected outcome before judging whether you failed to hold. — [The playbook summary (Google Slides)](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1NZV1JGhKXawqJ08dUdVPViufldl3CDUjgB8hadBWEho/htmlpresent)
- [A, primary] SMB's Winning Trader syllabus lists sessions on the "Changing Fundamentals Trade", the "2nd Day Play" and a separate VWAP module (titles only; mechanics not in the syllabus). — [SMB: The Winning Trader Syllabus (PDF)](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/The-Winning-Trader-Syllabus-Update.pdf)

**When the buyer is done / Reasons2Sell**
- [A, primary] Bellafiore: a break of the trader's trend line is a Reason2Sell; exit, re-evaluate, and possibly re-enter on a fresh excellent risk/reward setup rather than abandoning the stock. — [SMB: A Reason2Sell in $EPXR](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/?p=25165)
- [A, primary] Bellafiore: the desk builds lists of exit triggers — examples are a trendline break and reaching a significant long-term resistance level; visible weakness on the tape near your price target is also a Reason2Sell. — [SMB: An exit strategy to add to your trading (Reasons2Sell)](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/an-exit-strategy-to-add-to-your-trading-reasons2sell)
- [A, primary] A One Good Trade post frames trade management as deciding when to add, lighten up, cut, exit and re-evaluate, and cites new increasing volume entering after a steep down move as a cue to exit almost all of a (short) position. — [SMB: Making One Good Trade Decision](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/making-one-good-trade-decision)

**Adding size**
- [A, primary] SMBU trading tip (WWE): "Your position size should increase as new data confirming your trade appears", using VWAP as a guide and moving from Tier 1 to Tier 4. — [SMB: SMBU Trading Tip of the Week ($WWE)](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/smbu-trading-tip-of-the-week-wwe)
- [A, primary] Bellafiore: "Identify your trading strengths. Trade your best setups more often and with larger size." — [SMB: Here is how you trade bigger](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/here-is-how-you-trade-bigger)
- [A, primary] Bellafiore: raise size about ten percent for a few weeks, and raise again only if that works; you cannot jump from 500 to 3,000 shares by deciding to. — [SMB: trading size archive](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/tag/trading-size)
- [A, primary] From The PlayBook: rather than just adding shares, increase the account and intraday loss limit and "continue risking 30 percent of your intraday loss limit" on a trade. — [SMB: From The PlayBook: Trade a Bigger Account](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/from-the-playbook-trade-a-bigger-account)
- [A, primary] "Tier Size" post: most size on the open, least at midday, more than midday but less than the open into the close; cut size in slow or volatile conditions. — [SMB: Tier Size](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/tier-size)

### Inferences
- SMB's exit logic for a held-bid trade is two-stage rather than a single stop: the dropped bid is the exit, and the stock's failure to go down afterwards is the re-entry. That makes "the buyer is done" a judgement confirmed by price follow-through, not by the bid disappearing alone.
- SMB's tiering is information-driven (add when new confirming data appears, with VWAP as one reference), which is compatible with "add on the VWAP hold / reclaim" but SMB's excerpted text does not state that specific rule.
- The Day 2 play explicitly requires Day 1 to close near its high, so the Day 1 close location is doing double duty as SMB's overnight-continuation filter.

### Gaps
- No SMB primary text retrieved stating "long above VWAP, short below", "VWAP is the institutional benchmark", "VWAP reclaim" rules, or a 5-minute-close-below-VWAP exit. These remain **[C] for SMB attribution** until checked against the SMB VWAP module or videos.
- The phrases "earn the right to size up" and "scale in when the trade proves itself" were not found verbatim in any retrieved SMB source.
- Mechanics of the Changing Fundamentals Trade, the Trend Trend Trade exit, and any "Shark" (SMB desk trader) material were not retrievable. YouTube transcripts were not accessible.
- One Good Trade and The PlayBook were not read; the held-bid detail above rests on a reader's review.

## 2. Lance Breitstein (ex-Trillium)

### Takeaway
Breitstein's own public statement gives a short list of what defines a change of trend (trendline break, holding the new side of VWAP, break of prior bars, break of a moving average, exhaustion in price or volume); secondary summaries add that he will not be long a stock steadily holding below VWAP unless it capitulates, enters reversals on the right side of the V, and trails on prior-bar lows. Sizing detail is thin in public sources.

### Cited Findings
- [A, primary] Breitstein on X: "Ways to define change of trend: not an exhaustive list and how I'll use is situational... Breaking a trendline. Holding new side of VWAP. Break of prior bars. Break of a moving average. Capitulatory exhaustive price move OR exhaustive volume exchange." — [Breitstein on X](https://twitter.com/TheOneLanceB/status/1516798713271767040)
- [A, secondary] He treats VWAP as a trend gauge: will not be long a stock steadily holding below VWAP, nor short one steadily holding above it, barring capitulation; checks the higher timeframe so the trade aligns with the larger trend. — [TraderLion: Lance Breitstein's Simple Setup That Made Millions](https://traderlion.com/investing-champions/lance-breitsteins-simple-setup-that-made-millions/)
- [A, secondary] He sizes about 25% bigger when the intraday and daily trends align; kept a sizing notecard listing his A, B and C trades and how much to risk on each; warns against sizing so large that a failure damages your psychology. — [TraderLion](https://traderlion.com/investing-champions/lance-breitsteins-simple-setup-that-made-millions/); [Chat With Traders transcript summary](https://sozai.app/transcript/ultimate-day-trader-lance-breitstein/)
- [A, secondary] Research sheet: waits for a V-bottom on a two-minute chart, initial stop at the day's low, then trails; uses a predetermined stop on more than 90% of trades. — [CTE Research Sheet: Lance Breitstein](https://completetradersedge.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/CTE-Research-Sheet-Lance-Breitstein-market-wizards-next-generation.pdf)
- [A, secondary, low reliability] A third-party skill file describes the mechanics as: enter after the lowest bar once price breaks the prior bar's high, stop at the low of the move, and for longs trail the prior bar's low, raising the stop each time a bar holds a higher low. — [skillsmp capitulation mean reversion](https://skillsmp.com/creators/mahmoud20138/tradecraft/plugins-tradecraft-skills-capitulation-mean-reversion)
- [A, primary, context] Trillium hosted a three-hour interview with Breitstein in October 2024 covering trading concepts. — [Trillium on X](https://x.com/TRLM/status/1849467967659987218)

### Inferences
- His change-of-trend list doubles as an exit list for trend trades and an entry list for reversals; "holding the new side of VWAP" (not merely crossing it) is the VWAP element.

### Gaps
- No primary source retrieved for "exponential sizing on pocket aces" or for a trendline-break exit rule stated as his own; the CTE sheet's wording on the trailing stop ("prior day's low" versus prior bar's low) conflicts with the other summary and could not be resolved.
- No published statistics on his setups were found.

## 3. Brian Shannon (Alphatrends): VWAP, Anchored VWAP, 5-day MA, overnight holds

### Takeaway
Shannon's verifiable rules are directional filters: no longs below a declining 5-day moving average, no shorts above a rising one, and a stock above a rising anchored VWAP is "innocent until proven guilty." On overnight holds, what he actually describes is holding a reduced position after taking profits intraday, not a "strong close above VWAP" checklist — that checklist could not be verified.

### Cited Findings
- [A, primary] A stock below a declining 5-day moving average is not a long candidate; a stock above a rising one is not a short candidate; the 5-day MA is the short-term trend definer, with AVWAP used to refine entries and risk. — [Alphatrends](https://alphatrends.net/?p=660714)
- [A, primary] AVWAP is "the one line on your chart that tells you who is actually in control"; above a rising AVWAP the trend is "innocent until proven guilty." — [Alphatrends: Anchored VWAP explained](https://alphatrends.net/anchored-vwap/)
- [A, primary] "Don't Chase the Gap, Wait for VWAP": anchor VWAP to major news events, earnings gaps and trend reversals. — [Alphatrends: When, Where & Why to Set Anchored VWAP](https://alphatrends.net/archives/when-where-why-to-set-anchored-vwap-brian-shannon/)
- [A, primary interview] Overnight: he trims during the day and, if he has taken profits along the way, may hold a smaller piece (he cites 1,500 shares) overnight "from a position of strength"; holds as long as the stock makes higher highs and higher lows on his timeframe. — [Stockbsessed interview, Mar 2023](https://stockbsessed.substack.com/p/interview-brian-shannon-what-it-takes)
- [A, primary interview] His stated objection to overnight risk: a stock bought at 80 can open at 76 with no chance to control risk. — [CMT Association Fill The Gap, Episode 61](https://cmtassociation.org/podcast/fill-the-gap-episode-sixty-one-anchored-vwap-legend-brian-shannon-cmt/)
- [A, secondary] He layers overnight-session VWAP, week-to-date VWAP, an anchor from the high of the week and the NYSE open. — [Alphatrends: Brian on NinjaTrader 05-18-25](https://alphatrends.net/archives/podcast/brian-on-ninjatrader-05-18-25/)
- [A, secondary] "Pinch and handoff": a pinch is price squeezed between an upper AVWAP (from a high) and a lower AVWAP (from a low); after the break, a new AVWAP anchored at the breakout takes over as support or resistance. The phrase is "believed to have been coined" by Shannon per an indicator author. — [TradingView: Anchored VWAP Pinch & Handoff](https://www.tradingview.com/script/VYu6A3GB-Anchored-VWAP-Pinch-Handoff-Intervals-and-Signals/)

### Inferences
- Shannon's overnight criterion is effectively "has the trade already paid me, and is the trend intact," which is a risk-reduction rule rather than a close-location rule.

### Gaps
- The rule "hold overnight only on a strong close above rising VWAPs" could not be verified in any primary Shannon source — treat as **[C]** as attributed to him. His book *Maximum Trading Gains with Anchored VWAP* (2023) was not read.
- No backtest of AVWAP-from-earnings, the pinch, or the 5-day MA filter was found.

## 4. Other practitioners: Raschke, Aziz, Qullamaggie, Brooks, and others

### Takeaway
Raschke and Brooks supply the trend-day definitions; Aziz supplies time-of-day VWAP rules; Qullamaggie supplies the best-documented swing exit structure (partial after 3-5 days, trail a 10- or 20-day MA close). None of these sources provides a tested intraday signal for "institutions are accumulating."

### Cited Findings
- [A, primary] Raschke: "A trend day occurs when there is an expansion in the daily trading range and the open and close are near opposite extremes." — [Raschke: Capturing Trend Days](https://www.traderslog.com/?p=823)
- [A, secondary] Raschke paraphrased: open near one extreme, close near the other, only shallow pullbacks; she classifies the day as trend or reversion beforehand and warns against averaging into losers on trend days. — [MyPivots: Recognizing Trend Days](https://www.mypivots.com/Board/Topic/5756/1/recognizing-trend-days); [Options Trading IQ: Linda Raschke rules](https://optionstradingiq.com/linda-raschke-rules-for-trading/)
- [A, primary] Aziz (Bear Bull Traders slides): the VWAP false breakout is a late-morning setup (roughly 10:00-10:30 am in one deck, 10:00 am-12:00 pm in another); "Most VWAP trades should start a trend after. If they do not, they are likely false breakouts"; go short when a stock fails to hold above VWAP; "Better to wait for next 5 min for confirmation." Stocks in play should start a trend in the afternoon above or below VWAP. — [Bear Bull Traders Session 4 (PDF)](https://bearbulltraders.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Session-4.pdf); [Aziz: Advanced Techniques in Day Trading (PDF)](https://bearbulltraders.com/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/Advanced-Techniques-in-Day-Trading.pdf)
- [A, secondary] Qullamaggie: sell a third to a half after about 3-5 days, move the stop to breakeven, trail the rest on the 10-day (or 20-day for slower names) moving average, exiting on the first close below it. — [Qullamaggie Swing Trading School transcript summary](https://sozai.app/transcript/qullamaggie-swing-trading-school/index.md); [CTE Research Sheet: Kristjan Kullamagi](https://completetradersedge.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/CTE-Research-Sheet-Kristjan-Kullamagi-market-wizards-next-generation.pdf)
- [B, weak, third-party] A case-study backtest adapting Qullamaggie's breakout rules exited high-ADR stocks (above 10%) on a close below the 10-day EMA and lower-ADR stocks below the 20-day EMA, with no partials — so it does not test his actual method. Results not retrieved. — [Financial Wisdom TV case study](https://www.financialwisdomtv.com/post/qullamaggie-breakout-setup-case-study-what-the-top-100-winning-stocks-reveal)
- [A, primary] Brooks: "Trend from the Open and Small Pullback Trends" is a chapter of his book; his "always in" question is which side you would take if forced to be in the market; his forum states there is no fixed exit rule set. — [Brooks Trading Course: Always In](https://www.brookstradingcourse.com/?p=56246); [Brooks forum: sizing and profit taking](https://www.brookstradingcourse.com/support-forum/how-to-learn-price-action/sizing-and-profit-taking/)

### Inferences
- The practitioner definitions of a trend day (range expansion, open and close at opposite extremes, shallow pullbacks) are descriptive after the fact; none of these sources gives a tested real-time classifier.

### Gaps
- Nothing retrievable for Zanger, Minervini, Jeff Cooper, John Carter, Corey Rosenbloom, or the Trillium / T3 / Kershner blogs within this session. Any rule attributed to them should be treated as unverified.
- No source found that states Raschke holds trend-day positions into the close in her own words.
- No named practitioner source gave a tested intraday accumulation signal; the SMB held-bid material in section 1 is the closest.

## 5. Empirical evidence: VWAP trend, intraday momentum, ORB on Stocks in Play, SD bands

### Takeaway
Three Zarattini/Aziz/Barbon papers report strong backtests for (1) long-above/short-below VWAP on QQQ, (2) a volatility-band intraday momentum strategy on SPY with a VWAP trailing stop, and (3) a 5-minute opening-range breakout restricted to high relative-volume stocks. All are author-run backtests, one co-author runs a trading education business, and an independent critique says the VWAP result goes to break-even at one tick of slippage. I found no credible test of VWAP standard-deviation bands.

### Cited Findings

**Zarattini & Aziz — VWAP trend (SSRN 4631351)**
- [B] Rule: long when price is above session VWAP, short when below, flat at the close. Instruments QQQ and TQQQ. Sample 2 Jan 2018 to 28 Sep 2023. — [SSRN abstract 4631351](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4631351)
- [B] QQQ: $25,000 grew to $192,656 net of commissions (671%), maximum drawdown 9.4%, Sharpe 2.1, versus QQQ buy-and-hold of 126% with a 37% drawdown and Sharpe 0.7. TQQQ: $25,000 to $2,085,417 (8,242%, about 116% a year). — [SSRN abstract 4631351](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4631351); [Bear Bull Traders summary](https://bearbulltraders.com/?p=2606339)
- [B, critique] An independent review quotes the paper's assumption "we assumed no slippage in our order fills", reports the strategy breaks even at a single tick of slippage on QQQ, and gets a Sharpe of 1.91 rather than 2.1. — [Telonics Trading: Does the "Holy Grail" Paper Hold Up?](https://telonicstrading.com/research-vwap.html)
- [B, critique] A QuantConnect forum thread titled "not so fast" disputes the paper, and one QuantConnect "VWAP_trend" report shows a Sharpe of -0.6; details not readable. — [QuantConnect forum thread](https://www.quantconnect.com/forum/discussion/16706/bear-bull-traders-039-paper-on-quot-the-holy-grail-quot-not-so-fast/); [QuantConnect VWAP_trend report](https://www.quantconnect.com/reports/2dd44a5c1394e0ba3de9c7f4d7dc0716)

**Zarattini, Aziz & Barbon — "Beat the Market" SPY intraday momentum (SFI 24-97)**
- [B] Rule: build a "noise area" around the open from average intraday moves over the prior 14 days; enter in the direction of a break out of it, checked on the hour and half-hour; trail longs with the higher of the upper boundary or intraday VWAP (mirror for shorts); flat at the close. — [SFI publication 24-97](https://www.sfi.ch/de/publications/n-24-97-beat-the-market-an-effective-intraday-momentum-strategy-for-s-p500-etf-spy); [Bear Bull Traders Money Show deck](https://bearbulltraders.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Money_Show_Final.pdf)
- [B] Results 2007 to early 2024: total return 1,985% net of costs, 19.6% annualised, Sharpe 1.33. — [RePEc listing](https://ideas.repec.org/p/chf/rpseri/rp2497.html)
- [B, secondary] Maximum drawdown about -25% (May 2007-Apr 2024); costs assumed $0.0035/share commission plus $0.001/share slippage. — [CXO Advisory summary](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/momentum-investing/complex-intraday-time-series-momentum-strategy-applied-to-spy/)
- [B, secondary] A paper review reports a hit ratio around 43% for this strategy (i.e. it wins less than half the time and depends on large winners). — [Quant Macro paper review](https://quantmacro.substack.com/p/paper-review-an-effective-intraday)

**Zarattini, Barbon & Aziz — ORB on Stocks in Play (SSRN 4729284 / SFI 24-98)**
- [B] Tested opening-range breakout on more than 7,000 US stocks, 2016-2023; profits are largest with a 5-minute opening range; restricting to Stocks in Play (unusually high volume that day, usually news-driven) gives a significant benefit after transaction costs. — [RePEc listing](https://ideas.repec.org/p/chf/rpseri/rp2498.html); [Barbon paper page](https://abarbon.com/papers/a-profitable-day-trading-strategy-for-the-u-s-equity-market)
- [B, secondary] 5-minute ORB on the top 20 relative-volume stocks, stop at 10% of ATR, exit at the close: about 41.6% annualised versus roughly 15% for the S&P 500, Sharpe 2.81 versus 0.78, January 2016-December 2023. — [CXO Advisory summary](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/individual-investing/intraday-trading-of-overactive-stocks-via-opening-range-breakout/)
- [B, replication] A QuantConnect replication reports Sharpe 2.396 and beta -0.042, versus 0.836 Sharpe for SPY buy-and-hold. — [QuantConnect: Opening Range Breakout for Stocks in Play](https://www.quantconnect.com/forum/discussion/18444/Opening+Range+Breakout+for+Stocks+in+Play/p4/comment-50717)

**VWAP standard-deviation bands**
- [C] No peer-reviewed or independent test of +/-1 or +/-2 SD VWAP bands was found. A TradingView author claims VWAP mean reversion has a "sweet spot between 2 and 4 standard deviations" with roughly 50-bar holds, without independent verification. — [TradingView: Where the Edge Actually Lives](https://www.tradingview.com/chart/ES1!/Nor75pZD-Where-the-Edge-Actually-Lives/)
- [B, weak] One shared TrendSpider VWAP-band strategy returned 7.30% versus 10.50% buy-and-hold with a 69% win rate and 0.62 reward-to-risk — a single unaudited example that lost to buy-and-hold. — [TrendSpider shared strategy](https://charts.trendspider.com/shared/64c3d68a1e74100015bd88c5?t=1)

### Inferences
- The evidence that exists supports VWAP as a trend filter on index ETFs and relative volume as a stock-selection filter; it does not test the discretionary single-stock rules (VWAP reclaim, fade to VWAP, held bid) the user actually trades.
- The roughly 43% hit rate reported for the SPY strategy implies the VWAP-trailed trend approach earns its return from a minority of large trend days, consistent with the practitioner emphasis on holding winners.
- The slippage sensitivity of the QQQ VWAP result matters less for a trader taking a handful of selective trades than for a system that flips at every VWAP cross, but it does mean the paper's headline numbers should not be quoted as achievable.

### Gaps
- The papers' full methodology (bar size, sizing, leverage, trade counts, win rates, long/short split) could not be read; the SSRN PDFs need to be checked directly.
- No test found of the "ride the +1 SD band on trend days, fade the +2 SD band on range days" practitioner heuristic — it is **[C]**.
- All three papers end in 2023 or early 2024; no out-of-sample update through 2026 was found.

### Takeaway
The documented exit rules are structural (trendline break, loss of the held bid, tape weakness at a target, exhaustion volume, holding the wrong side of VWAP) rather than indicator recipes. The specific recipes the user listed — 5-minute close below VWAP, 9/21 EMA trailing on 2- or 5-minute bars, time stops — were not found attributed to a named credible source.

## 7. Overnight holds: criteria and evidence

### Takeaway
Practitioner criteria that could be sourced are: Day 1 closes near the high on a breakout or fundamental catalyst (SMB), the trade is trending on both timeframes (SMB Trend-Trend), and the position has already been reduced for profit (Shannon). The academic evidence is mixed for single stocks: momentum profits accrue overnight, but individual stocks show end-of-day reversal, and no study was found that directly tests "closes at the high above VWAP on heavy relative volume."

### Cited Findings
- [A, primary] SMB: the Day 2 continuation play requires a powerful directional Day 1 on a breakout or fundamental catalyst, closing near the high of the range. — [SMB: Ultimate Swing Trading Guide](https://www.smbtraining.com/blog/the-ultimate-swing-trading-guide-for-beginners-developing-traders)
- [A, primary] SMB: the best 2nd Day Play is a Trend-Trend trade (trending on both long and short timeframes). — [SMB: 2nd Day Play presentation](https://www.slideshare.net/smbcapital/edu-2nd-day-play-presentation)
- [A, primary interview] Shannon: holds a reduced position overnight after taking profits, as long as higher highs and higher lows persist. — [Stockbsessed interview](https://stockbsessed.substack.com/p/interview-brian-shannon-what-it-takes)
- [B] Lou, Polk & Skouras (Journal of Financial Economics, 2019): across 14 strategies, abnormal profits are earned either entirely overnight (momentum and reversal variants) or entirely intraday, typically with opposite signs; the working paper says all abnormal momentum returns occur overnight. The authors caution that transaction costs make exploiting this far less attractive. — [LSE Research Online](https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/87481); [Alpha Architect summary](https://alphaarchitect.com/2015/02/overnight-momentum-vs-intraday-momentum/)
- [B] Gao, Han, Li & Zhou (JFE, 2018): for the S&P 500 ETF, 1993-2013, the first half-hour return predicts the last half-hour return (predictive R-squared 1.6%). This is market-level, not stock-level. — [Alpha Architect summary](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/08/attention-prop-traders-the-first-half-hour-of-trading-predicts-the-last-half-hour/); [EconPapers listing](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jfinec:v:129:y:2018:i:2:p:394-414)
- [B] A stock-level study reports an "end-of-day reversal" pattern for individual stocks, in contrast to market-level intraday momentum. — [Notre Dame working paper (EOD)](https://www3.nd.edu/~zda/EOD.pdf)

### Inferences
- The Lou-Polk-Skouras result is directionally supportive of holding momentum names overnight rather than only intraday, but it is a monthly-rebalanced cross-sectional result, not a test of a single-day strong-close signal.
- The stock-level end-of-day reversal finding is a caution against assuming a strong last half-hour in a single stock predicts further gains.

### Gaps
- No study found that tests the practitioner checklist directly (close in the top of the day's range, above VWAP all day, high relative volume, fresh catalyst) against next-day returns. Gap-and-go Day 2 statistics and post-earnings-drift evidence were not researched within the tool budget.
- Zanger and Minervini overnight/strong-close rules were not retrievable.
