## 1. TTWO around Grand Theft Auto V (launch September 17, 2013)

### Takeaway
TTWO ran up about 57% from late January to a late-August 2013 high, then faded into launch, fell 2% on launch day and was still about 2.4% below its pre-launch close 20 trading days later, despite a record $1 billion in three days. The big gains (about +35% at one year, +163% at three, +673% at five) came later, as GTA Online spending built, from a starting valuation of about 1.4x sales.

### Cited Findings
- Pre-launch run-up: TTWO gained about 57% from a January 31, 2013 close of $12.17 to an August 26, 2013 pre-launch high of $19.10, then retreated before launch and "did not establish a sustained upward trend immediately after GTA V arrived." — [FXCentrum](https://fxcentrum.com/ttwo-stock/ttwo-stock-when-gta-5-released-before-and-after-gta-v-analysis/)
- Close the day before launch (September 16, 2013): $17.35. — [FXCentrum](https://fxcentrum.com/ttwo-stock/ttwo-stock-when-gta-5-released-before-and-after-gta-v-analysis/)
- Launch day (September 17, 2013): closed 2.0% lower at $17.00. Twenty trading days later the stock stood at 97.6% of its pre-launch close (about 2.4% below, which implies roughly $16.93). — [CryptoTicker](https://cryptoticker.io/en/take-two-stock-gta-6/)
- Short-term path: roughly +4% after five trading days, and a September 30, 2013 close of $18.17. — [FXCentrum](https://fxcentrum.com/ttwo-stock/ttwo-stock-when-gta-5-released-before-and-after-gta-v-analysis/); [CryptoTicker](https://cryptoticker.io/en/take-two-stock-gta-6/)
- A different source gives $16.99 for three days after launch. — [Admiral Markets](https://admiralmarkets.com/education/articles/shares/take-two-stock-analysis)
- Sales announcement: Take-Two said on September 20, 2013 that GTA V passed $1 billion in retail sales in its first three days, the fastest entertainment product to do so (Call of Duty: Black Ops 2 took 15 days). The figure was based on the company's internal estimates. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2013-09-20/take-two-says-grand-theft-auto-sales-top-1-billion-in-3-days); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2013/09/20/gta-v-1-billion-in-3-days/)
- Longer-run returns for a buyer after the September 2013 launch: about +35% in one year, +163% in three years, +673% in five years. — [Yahoo Finance / Motley Fool](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/gta-vi-days-away-dont-223102638.html)
- A hypothetical $10,000 bought on GTA V launch day (588 shares) was worth $131,994 as of mid-November 2026. — [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/16/gta-vi-comes-out-thursday-she-bought-588-take-two-shares-the-day-gta-v-launched-theyre-worth-131994/)
- Valuation then versus now: TTWO traded at about 1.4x sales at the GTA V launch versus 5.7x before GTA VI ships. To repeat the GTA V returns, market value would have to go from about $39 billion to $102 billion in three years and $300 billion in five. — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/11/gta-vi-days-away-don-t-expect-gta-v-style-returns-from-take-two-stock-at-5-7-times-sales/)
- The stock rose 11.7% between GTA V's 2011 announcement and its release. — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2023/09/21/is-take-two-interactive-stock-a-buy-now)
- 2013 calendar year: year-end close $17.37, annual change +57.77%. — [Macrotrends](https://www.macrotrends.net/stocks/charts/TTWO/take-two-interactive-software/stock-price-history)
- An individual chart note (low reliability) gives $17.37 at release and $21.25 six months later. — [TradingView user post](https://www.tradingview.com/chart/TTWO/MPbixosQ-TTWO-Bearish-play/)
- GTA Online launched October 1, 2013 with server errors, connection failures and loading problems; Rockstar apologised and said it was working around the clock. — [The Guardian via inkl](https://www.inkl.com/news/rockstar-says-sorry-for-grand-theft-auto-online-launch-problems); [VideoGamer](https://www.videogamer.com/news/rockstar-working-around-the-clock-to-resolve-gta-online-issues)

### Inferences
- The GTA V record is a mild "sell the news" case: the high ($19.10) was set about three weeks before launch, the launch-day close ($17.00) was about 11% below that high, and the stock went nowhere for the first month even with a record sales headline three days in.
- A +35% one-year return from the $17.00 launch close implies about $23 in September 2014. The six-month figure of $21.25 (low-reliability source) would be about +25%. So the payoff from GTA V was real but arrived over quarters, not days.
- The year-end 2013 close of $17.37 is essentially flat with the launch-eve close of $17.35, so the first three and a half months after launch produced roughly nothing.
- The valuation starting point is the largest difference from today: 1.4x sales in 2013 versus 5.7x now, so the multi-year GTA V return is not a fair template.

### Gaps
- Exact closes at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months after launch were not obtained from a price table. Only the 20-trading-day figure, the 2013 year-end close, and the approximate one-year percentage are sourced.
- No sourced account was found of the one-day stock reaction to the September 18 ($800 million first day) and September 20 ($1 billion) announcements. The $800 million first-day figure itself was not surfaced by the searches run.
- No source found tying a specific date or quarter to the re-rating on recurrent spending, and none describing any stock reaction to the rocky GTA Online start.
- Broader-market comparison for September 2013 to September 2014 was not sourced.

## 2. TTWO around Red Dead Redemption 2 (launch October 26, 2018)

### Takeaway
RDR2 is the clearest TTWO "sell the news" case: the stock peaked in early October 2018, fell 7.4% on the first trading day after launch, bounced up to 12% on the $725 million announcement, then slid about 15% in November and a further 13-17% in February 2019 on guidance. The October-December leg overlapped a broad market selloff, but the November and February drops were tied to Take-Two's own earnings outlook.

### Cited Findings
- By the time of an InvestorPlace piece, the stock had fallen about 27% from its early-October 2018 highs. — [InvestorPlace](https://investorplace.com/?p=1296253)
- Launch-day prices (October 26, 2018): $121.72 intraday per Schaeffer's; a $120.70 close reported around launch. — [Schaeffer's](https://schaeffersresearch.com/content/analysis/2018/10/26/take-two-stock-could-have-gas-in-the-tank); [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/?p=369640)
- The stock lost 7.4% on the first trading day after launch and stood at 91.5% of its pre-launch close 20 trading days after launch. — [CryptoTicker](https://cryptoticker.io/en/take-two-stock-gta-6/)
- Take-Two stock sold off after the October 26 release, then posted big gains after the October 30 press release that RDR2 made over $725 million in its first three days; shares jumped as much as 12.3% on October 30. Piper Jaffray had projected $400-500 million. — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2018/10/30/ttwo.aspx); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2018/10/30/red-dead-redemption-2-sales-record/)
- November 2018: shares fell 14.9% in the month after the fiscal second-quarter report in early November; the company beat on revenue and profit but investors wanted a stronger outlook to justify the valuation. — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2018/12/08/why-take-two-interactive-stock-dropped-15-in-novem.aspx)
- February 6, 2019: shares fell about 13-15% on the day to about $93.72, despite RDR2 shipping 23 million copies and quarterly net bookings of $1.57 billion versus guidance of $1.40-1.45 billion, because fourth-quarter guidance (EPS of 67-77 cents) disappointed. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/red-dead-redemption-2-gallops-to-23-million-copies-in-a-quarter-stock-falls-13/); [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/content/take-twos-stock-falls-despite-huge-red-dead-redemption-2-sales)
- The same day EA also plunged; coverage tied the sector slide to weak forecasts and competition from Fortnite. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2019-02-06/take-two-slumps-on-outlook-another-major-miss-for-game-makers); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2019/02/06/electronic-arts-take-two-earnings/)
- February 2019 as a whole: stock dropped about 17%, attributed to the company failing to convince investors that the next year's growth justified the valuation. — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2019/03/06/why-take-two-interactive-stock-dropped-17-in-febru.aspx)
- A later source says the stock rose another 181% after RDR2's launch (window unspecified). — [Yahoo Finance / Motley Fool](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/gta-vi-days-away-dont-223102638.html)

### Inferences
- From about $121 at launch to about $93.72 on February 6, 2019 is a decline of roughly 23% in a little over three months, after a record launch that beat the sell-side sales estimate by about 50%.
- Market versus company: the October-December 2018 period was a broad US equity selloff (not sourced here), which likely explains part of the pre-launch slide from the early-October high. But the two sharpest legs (November -14.9%, February 6 -13% to -15%) happened on earnings days and were explained by guidance and valuation; the February leg occurred when the broad market was recovering. The sell-off was also sector-wide (EA, Activision), with Fortnite cited as a common factor.
- The pattern in both TTWO cases: a record launch did not lift the stock; the next quarterly guidance did the damage or the repair.

### Gaps
- The exact early-October 2018 record high (commonly recalled as about $139) and its date were not confirmed by any search result.
- The exact trough price and date in early 2019, and closes at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months after launch, were not obtained.
- No sourced S&P 500 / Nasdaq figures for the same windows, so the market-versus-stock split is qualitative.

## 3. Other analogs: Activision, Electronic Arts, CD Projekt, Nintendo

### Takeaway
The other cases mostly show peaks before or at launch followed by declines: Activision fell on a record Black Ops 4 opening, CD Projekt lost about 40% in two weeks and over 60% within months after a broken Cyberpunk 2077, and Nintendo peaked two months after Switch 2 launched and then roughly halved. EA around Battlefield 6 is not a usable analog because a pending buyout pinned the price.

### Cited Findings
Activision Blizzard (Call of Duty)
- Black Ops 4 (launched October 12, 2018): Activision fell as much as 9% on October 18, 2018 after announcing more than $500 million of sales in the first three days, because the figure did not improve on the 2017 launch and investors expected more. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2018-10-18/activision-slips-as-call-of-duty-launch-sales-may-disappoint); [InvestorPlace](https://investorplace.com/?p=1269976)
- Activision stock lost about 17% in October 2018, including an 11.8% drop in the latter part of the month. — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2018/11/07/why-activision-blizzard-stock-lost-17-in-october.aspx)
- Modern Warfare 3 (2011) grossed $400 million in the US and UK in its first 24 hours. — [Activision Blizzard wiki mirror](https://www.doc.surf/wiki/activision-blizzard)

Electronic Arts (Battlefield 6, launched October 10, 2025)
- EA shares rose as much as 4.7% and reached a record high in August 2025 on the Battlefield 6 open beta (over 500,000 concurrent Steam players in its first weekend). — [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2025/08/eas-stock-reaches-a-record-high-on-the-back-of-explosive-battlefield-6-open-beta); [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/why-battlefield-6-is-driving-ea-stock-higher-202508120549)
- EA stock barely moved on Battlefield 6's launch day despite a huge player count, because EA had agreed to be acquired by PIF, Silver Lake and Affinity Partners in a deal valuing it at $55 billion. — [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/battlefield-6-player-count-surges-as-electronic-arts-ea-prepares-to-go-private)

CD Projekt (Cyberpunk 2077, launched December 10, 2020)
- Shares had risen more than 1,800% in five years to a pre-launch peak in early December 2020, then lost about 40% in a fortnight, erasing almost $5 billion of market value. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2020-12-19/futuristic-cyberpunk-gets-stuck-in-2020-as-bugs-derail-launch)
- On December 18, 2020, after Sony pulled the game from the PlayStation Store and offered refunds, shares fell 16-19% intraday. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2020/12/18/sony-pulls-cyberpunk-2077-from-playstation-store-after-backlash.html); [Business Recorder / Reuters](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40042567)
- The stock was down 40% from December 4, 2020 within weeks. — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/marketing/item-3197)
- By May 2021 shares were more than 60% below the pre-launch all-time high; later the stock was worth about a quarter of its pre-release level. — [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/2021/05/31/cd-projekt-profits-drop-as-cyberpunk-2077-remains-off-playstation-store); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/cd-projekt-stock-is-worth-a-quarter-of-what-it-was-before-cyberpunk-2077-released)

Nintendo (Switch 2, launched June 5, 2025)
- Shares fell on the January 2025 reveal (about 5-7% in Tokyo). — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2025/1/17/nintendo-shares-tumble-as-switch-2-reveal-disappoints-fans); [Benzinga](https://feeds2.benzinga.com/general/gaming/25/01/43051841/nintendo-shares-drop-5-on-tokyo-exchange-after-switch-2-announcement)
- Record high close of ¥14,795 in August 2025 (about two months after launch); by January 13, 2026 the close was ¥9,950, the first time below ¥10,000 since April 2025. — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/nintendo-share-price-falls-33-in-japan-amid-market-concerns)
- February 4, 2026: shares slid about 10-11% in a day on Switch 2 momentum fears. — [Reuters via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/nintendo-shares-slide-10-momentum-021900376.html)
- May 2026: shares closed 8.4% lower at ¥7,020, the lowest since August 2024, after Switch 2 price increases and worries about a thin game pipeline. — [ARY News / Reuters](https://arynews.tv/nintendo-shares-slump-as-price-hikes-games-shortfall-spook-market)
- June 10, 2026: shares fell 7.5% after a Nintendo Direct lacked top-franchise titles; down about a third year to date at that point. — [The Star / Reuters](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/06/10/nintendo-shares-slump-as-games-pipeline-disappoints-market)

### Inferences
- Nintendo's record close (¥14,795, August 2025) to the May 2026 close (¥7,020) is a fall of about 53% within eleven months of a commercially strong hardware launch. The peak came about two months after launch, not before it.
- CD Projekt is the downside tail: about -40% in two weeks and more than -60% in five months when the product was broken. GTA VI quality risk is different in kind, but this shows how quickly a stock priced on multi-year anticipation can unwind.
- Across five usable cases (TTWO GTA V, TTWO RDR2, ATVI Black Ops 4, CD Projekt Cyberpunk, Nintendo Switch 2), none showed a sustained rally in the first month after launch; four of the five were lower one to three months later. This is a small, hand-picked sample, not a statistical base rate.
- EA / Battlefield 6 should be excluded from any average: the buyout offer capped the price.

### Gaps
- No stock-reaction data found for Modern Warfare 3 (2011), Modern Warfare (2019) or other Call of Duty launches, so a consistent Activision pattern is not established.
- EA's per-share deal price (widely reported as $210) was not confirmed by the search results; EA around Battlefield V (2018) and Battlefield 2042 (2021) was not researched.
- Nintendo's launch-day (June 5, 2025) price and move were not found.
- CD Projekt's exact peak price (in zloty) and date were not found; Warsaw index moves over the same period not sourced.
- Other candidates (Ubisoft, Sony around PS5, Microsoft around Halo) were not researched.

### Inferences
- From the July 6, 2026 closing high ($258.41) to the October 30 low ($183.86 intraday) is a drawdown of about 29%, before the launch. Much of any "sell the rumor" unwinding may therefore already have happened, unlike GTA V and RDR2 where the stock was within about 11-13% of its high at launch.
- Monday's 6.3% fall was about 2.5 times the Nasdaq's 2.5% fall, so roughly 2.5 points were market and nearly 4 points stock-specific (high beta or pre-launch de-risking).
- If the $207.45 figure for November 17 is correct, the stock has given back the whole post-earnings rally and sits at the November 9 close ($207.40), about 20% below the July high and about 13% above the October 30 low.

## 5. Current (November 2026) commentary on sell-the-news risk, short interest and positioning

### Takeaway
Sell-side analysts remain overwhelmingly bullish (consensus targets about $277-291, roughly 30-40% above the price), while independent commentators flag that a launch which merely meets un-raised guidance could disappoint, and cite the GTA V and RDR2 precedents. Short interest is modest at about 5% of float, so there is neither a crowded short to squeeze nor heavy bearish positioning.

### Inferences
- The debate now is not whether GTA VI sells well (pre-orders say it will) but whether results can beat a launch-quarter guide of $3.35-3.50 billion and a full-year guide that was not raised. - For short-dated calls specifically: in both TTWO precedents the stock was lower 20 trading days after launch (-2.4% and -8.5%), and the sales-milestone press release (day 3-4) produced at best a brief bounce.
