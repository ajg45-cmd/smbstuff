## 1. Demand: pre-orders, pricing, forecasts, install base, comparisons

### Takeaway
Third-party data (Sensor Tower) puts digital console pre-orders at ~15.9M units / ~$1.54B as of Nov 17, with ~89% on the $99.99 Ultimate Edition; Take-Two says only that pre-orders are "far above" expectations and has deliberately NOT raised guidance for them. Forecasts are extremely dispersed (day one 15M-46M units; week one ~28M-51M; first year 35M-70M), and the base of comparison is GTA V's ~$800M day one / $1B in three days and RDR2's $725M three-day opening.

### Inferences
- If Sensor Tower is roughly right, digital pre-orders alone (~$1.5B gross consumer spend) already exceed GTA V's entire three-day gross ($1B) and are ~2x its day-one figure, so "beats GTA V day one" is close to a foregone conclusion; the real debate is the magnitude versus the very wide forecast range and versus what the stock already discounts.
- 15.9M digital pre-orders against an installed base of ~123-126M is ~13% attach before launch; analyst first-year unit forecasts of 35-45M imply ~28-36% attach, and Newzoo's 51M week-one implies ~40% in a week, which looks aggressive against the base.
- The 89% Ultimate mix lifts blended ASP to roughly $97-98 (my arithmetic: 0.89 x $99.99 + 0.11 x $79.99), which is consistent with $1.54B / 15.88M = ~$97. Pre-order buyers likely skew to the most committed fans, so the mix should drift toward Standard after launch.
- Q3 guidance of $3.35-3.5B net bookings was set without pre-order upside, by the company's own statement, so the guide is likely conservative, but that is also widely understood by the market.

### Gaps
- No official pre-order or unit figure from Take-Two/Rockstar; everything is Sensor Tower's model, methodology undisclosed in what I could read.
- I could not open primary sell-side notes; the analyst unit forecasts come via aggregators (Axis Intelligence and similar) and some pre-date the two delays. Dates of each forecast are unverified.
- No reliable source found for retail (code-in-box) pre-orders, non-US regional split, or Take-Two's net revenue share of the gross figure.
- "Refunds included" wording on the Sensor Tower figure was not clarified in what I saw.
- No confirmed console bundle found; no Sony/Microsoft statement on hardware supply.

There is no credible public number for the GTA VI marketing budget. What is documented is that a notable share of the visible campaign is paid for or co-funded by others or is revenue-generating for the host: Rockstar pays Miami-Dade County ~$3.9M (after the county pitched Rockstar), Sony funds PlayStation co-marketing, and the Heat "A Night in Vice City" game is a partnership whose financial terms are undisclosed in everything I could access.
