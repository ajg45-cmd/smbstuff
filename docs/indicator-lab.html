How they interact

The decision chain

The gates run left to right. Pace asks whether the stock is in play at all. The flush meter asks whether the stretch is in your sweet spot. The seller flag is the hard gate: nothing to its right matters until the flow has stopped. Room sets size. After entry the bounce clock and VWAP take over the exit. On one-way days the chain is skipped and the one-sided counter and give-back tracker run the trade.

Pine scripts to install

Two scripts. The dashboard puts all ten gauges and the decision chain in one table on your chart, with the 9 EMA, the anchored VWAP, turn markers and alerts. The pace gauge is a separate pane. Both are untested drafts: I cannot compile Pine from here, so expect to fix a small error or two and check each reading by hand before trusting it.


  

  

    
Install and use

    
    
      
  1. Open a 1- or 2-minute chart. Extended hours can stay on: the scripts count regular-session bars only.
    2. 
      
  2. Open the Pine Editor, paste a script, save it, and add it to the chart.

  3. Load at least 20 sessions of history so pace has a curve to compare against.
    4. 
      
  4. Turn on Bar Replay and step through a past session. The table updates bar by bar, the same way the simulator above does.

  5. Set the context flags and low-float mode in the script settings for each name.
